# Design documents

Each document explains one decision that is still in force: the context, the decision, why, the alternatives, the
consequences, where it is in the code, and links to the log entries. The decision logs
([../decisions/](../decisions/README.md)) are the archive.

Engine, rules, fork and protocol decisions are in the engine repository's design documents
([`docs/design/`](https://github.com/abstractclass-org/engines/blob/main/docs/design/README.md) and
[`v2/docs/design/`](https://github.com/abstractclass-org/engines/blob/main/v2/docs/design/README.md)). This folder
holds product decisions.

| # | Document | In short |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | [A game analysis tool for human players](01-analysis-tool.md) | What the tool does and why, from Lichess, KaTrain, Maia, KataGo and papers. Points lost, fitted thresholds and difficulty. A human model, puzzles, tags, distilled rules and concepts. |
| 02 | [A static site, with the analysis on the player's CPU](02-static-site.md) | Static files. The engine as versioned artifacts from the engine repository. One module instance per Web Worker. Heavy work offline on the GPU. An engine interface of our own design. |
| 03 | [The stack](03-stack.md) | TypeScript, SvelteKit (static), Vite, Cloudflare. Engine artifacts pinned by SHA-256: releases for deploys, local builds for development. |
| 04 | [The product's shape](04-product-shape.md) | Static now, a server later. Explanations from templates over engine facts. Desktop first, phone usable. Plain and clear, with a few rewards. |
| 05 | [The review's analysis](05-review-analysis.md) | KaTrain's schedule (rules, a sweep, deeper where the player looks), results kept in the browser. |
| 06 | [The review page's layout](06-review-layout.md) | The board fills the height, less the charts under it. Lichess's columns: the summary, the board, and the analysis from 1,260 px, with tabs below that. The win chance as a bar (the default) or a gauge. The display settings in a panel in the analysis column. No text selection. |
| 07 | [The engine's lines and the player's variations](07-lines-and-variations.md) | A candidate's line on the board on hover (KaTrain's), numbered, one edge at a time, with its cuts and regions. One line listed under the table, which a click plays as a branch. The game as a tree (Lichess's), its branches in the move list. The player's own moves by clicks or a drag, and notes drawn on the board. |
| 08 | [Move sounds](08-sound.md) | A sound for an edge and another for an edge that cuts. A button by default, or a pencil stroke, chosen in the display settings. A sound plays on a step forward and on a move played or clicked. Jumps are silent. On by default, with a switch and a volume. |
| 09 | [The engine's settings](09-engine-settings.md) | The engine's switch and gear in the engine header (Lichess's engine box). The position on screen's visits and time limit. The review's depth (Lite, Standard, Custom). The network by its name and its download. The workers and the GPU. Kept in this browser, and applied without a reload. |
| 10 | [How a turn or an edge gets its label](10-labels.md) | Every label rule in one place. Labels by turn (the default) or by edge, and losses across positions. Chess.com's bands on the engine's win chance. Best, Great and the special cases. |
| 11 | [Early access: Hedge School's review on the web](11-early-access.md) | The review only, as Hedge School, at hedgeschool.abstractclass.org. One build switch and no sample game. Imports from this site's notation, Constellation's export and the meaf.us bookmarklet. Listed, on Cloudflare, with the engine from a release, two analytics and the notation page. |
| 12 | [The logo, the mascot and the colour](12-logo.md) | A Border Collie in black and white, traced from generated drawings, and lavender as the school's colour: the site's accent and the partners' buttons. The head alone below 48 pixels (the header, the favicon), and the head and chest from 48 pixels up (the About page). Neither turns into a negative on a dark page. |
| 13 | [Export and import of reviewed games](13-export-import.md) | The review file holds the record, the branches, the engine's results and the finished review. It opens at once where the engine is the same. The annotated record holds evaluations, labels, marks and branches as text. Only a finished review exports. |
| 14 | [A position's difficulty: hard positions and the accuracy's weights](14-complexity.md) | KaTrain's complexity in the engine's win chance. ◆ marks a hard position. The accuracy weighs each move by KaTrain's weight. A Difficulty chart, and a meter of the likely edges after Maia's. Every number step by step. |
| 15 | [The review on the visitor's GPU](15-gpu.md) | The GPU worker as one more worker: 16 searches batched through WebGPU. A task takes a CPU worker first. Auto tests the GPU on the review's own work and keeps it where it is faster than a worker. Large files stored compressed, in parts. |
| 16 | [How deep a review searches, which result a position uses, and whose settings apply](16-depths-and-settings.md) | Every depth a review asks for, in one table: the sweep, the depth per move, the key moments at twice it, the Greats' equal searches, and the position on screen. Each position uses its deepest result, in the page and from the store. A game keeps the settings it came with. What a review file and a share link carry. Ten worked examples. |
| 17 | [What the site measures, and the decisions it serves](17-measurement.md) | Events to Google Analytics, one for each decision: imports and their failures, the review's start and end with its settings and time, the GPU's test, settings, features, exports. Counts and ranges only, never a game. The review's address names its game after the `#`. The setup in Google Analytics, and the reports that answer each question. |
| 18 | [Links from other sites, and reviews saved back](18-partner-links.md) | A site where Enclosure is played links a finished game to `/review#record=…` (first `/import#record=…`, design 22): the game is kept in the browser and its review opens, with a link back to the game's page from its `Site` tag. Other variants are refused. A partner's link names its save page: the Export menu's "Save to …" sends the review file there by window messages, when the player asks, and the partner's page sends it back the same way to open it again. The link and the handshake are described on the /developers page, with what can change. |
| 19 | [Hedge School's place](19-position.md) | A school for every play site: it reviews, explains and teaches (lessons, puzzles, studies), and runs no play. Its edge is teaching, not the review alone. Static files and the browser first, a light backend on Cloudflare only when a feature needs shared data, and human games by agreement. |
| 20 | [A review by an earlier engine opens as it was](20-snapshot.md) | After a release, a game reviewed before opens as a snapshot of that review, from the stored results: no search, no new moves, no export, and Review again. Without the earlier results, the game is reviewed again with a notice. Imported files and reviews kept by a partner open the same way. |
| 21 | [The Analysis board](21-analysis.md) | A free board beside the review: the standard start or a line brought in, the engine on the position on screen only, the player's main line (Make main line), labels only on request, one board kept in each browser, shared by a link or as a record. The board editor and starts from another position wait for the engine's check of a position (request 16). |
| 22 | [The site's pages and their addresses](22-site-map.md) | Each page takes its own way in after the `#`: `/review#record=`, `#packed=`, `#from=`, and `/analysis#record=`, `#packed=`, `&at=`. Parameters are named by their payload's format. `/import` is deprecated, and forwards to `/review` during the early access. The meaf.us tools' page is `/tools`. The analytics start once a page has taken a game out of its address. |
| 23 | [Search engines and link previews](23-search.md) | The live address is indexed, Cloudflare's preview addresses are not. Each page has its title, description, canonical address and Open Graph tags, with one preview image. A sitemap, structured data on Home, and the site registered in Google Search Console. |
| 24 | [Practice: a game against HedgeFund in the browser](24-practice.md) | `/practice`: a game against the engine's practice bot in the player's browser, with no server and no clock. The network, the visits and the variety, fixed for a game. Blue, Red or Random. No evaluation. Take back by turn, Abort at any time, Resign, and steps back through the positions. The review's row, move list, bar and display settings. One game in progress, kept. Games go into Your games, named by both sides. |
| 25 | [Practice games, stored for the bot's levels](25-practice-games-stored.md) | Every practice game stored when it ends, on a Cloudflare Pages Function and D1, as Lichess keeps every game: its moves, the bot, the outcome, the takebacks and the times, never a name or an IP address. A random number per browser with the visitor's consent, and a choice for each purpose. May be published as an open dataset. Built: it stores games once the databases are set up. |
| 26 | [One repository for the learning sites](26-one-repository.md) | Hedge School moves whole into `apps/hedgeschool`, beside a hub at `abstractclass.org` (a page of links, no analytics). Nothing shared in `packages/` until two sites need it. The addresses, the storage keys and this repository stay. Not built yet. |
